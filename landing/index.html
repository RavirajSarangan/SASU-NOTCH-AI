A guard that knows your Mac

Nova Guard learns what's normal on your Mac and tells you the moment something changes: a new admin account, a remote login, an unknown startup item, another app using your microphone.

  • Safe fixes happen on their own and can be undone: risky startup items go to quarantine, reverse shells are stopped.
  • Fixes that need admin rights wait for your click and your password, and show the exact command first.
  • It's honest about its limits: no app can promise complete protection, so Guard watches the ways people most often get in.

What Nova Guard watches

Checks run on your Mac every few minutes. Alerts name the app or account involved, and every automatic fix can be undone.

Who gets in

  • Remote Login (SSH) and Screen Sharing sessions, with where they come from
  • Repeated failed login attempts
  • New user or admin accounts, and admin commands (sudo)
  • New SSH keys that can log in to your Mac

What starts by itself

  • New startup items; risky ones are moved to quarantine (undoable)
  • New configuration profiles and scheduled jobs (cron)
  • New admin rules in /etc/sudoers.d
  • Changes to your shell startup files

Your Mac's defences

  • FileVault disk encryption, Firewall and stealth mode
  • Gatekeeper and System Integrity Protection
  • Automatic security updates and the screen-lock password
  • A score out of 100, with one-click fixes

Camera, microphone and network

  • Which app turned on the camera or microphone, by name
  • New ports open to the network, and unverified apps installed
  • macOS's own speech services are recognised, so there's no false alarm

How it works

  1. Your agent runs a hook. A tiny helper is added to Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Gemini. It sends each step to Nova.
  2. Over a private local socket. Only your user account can connect. Nothing goes to a server.
  3. Nova shows it in the notch. Your answers go back the same way, so the agent carries on instantly.

Private by default

Stays on your Mac: agent activity, security checks, your voice, the beat of your music, and your settings.

Leaves only when you ask: chat questions and drafts go to the AI provider you chose, with your own key.

Never: analytics, tracking or telemetry. Keys live in the Keychain, not in files.

Bring Nova home

macOS 15 or later. Works on MacBooks with a notch and in the menu bar on other Macs.

Get the source on GitHub

Nova is open source (MIT). Build it with scripts/build_app.sh. It isn't notarized yet, so the first time, right-click the app and choose Open.

Questions

Does Nova slow down my Mac?

No. Nova draws its animations outside the normal layout work, skips frames when nothing visibly changes, and pauses when the notch is hidden or your screen is locked.

Can Nova approve commands on its own?

Never. Nova can deny dangerous commands automatically, but allowing always needs your click, and high-risk commands need two. Voice can deny, never approve.

Is Nova always listening?

Only if you turn on “Hey Nova”. Then it listens on your Mac only, shows that it's listening, and pauses while your screen is locked or another app is using the microphone. Otherwise it listens only while you hold ⌥ Space.

Does it understand Tamil?

Yes, for the built-in commands, including Tanglish like “paattu podu” or “enna nadakkuthu”. Tamil speech recognition depends on your macOS version supporting it offline; Settings tells you if it does.

Will Nova change my system settings?

Only safe, undoable fixes in your own account happen automatically. Anything that needs admin rights waits for you and shows the exact command before macOS asks for your password.

Which agents are supported?

Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, Antigravity and VS Code. Any tool can report to Nova's local socket.
