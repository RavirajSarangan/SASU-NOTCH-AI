Where Nova leads

  • Watches your Mac's security and fixes safe things itself, with undo.
  • Always denies dangerous agent commands, and asks twice before high-risk ones.
  • A character drawn live with 18 moods that reacts to your agents, and dances to your music.
  • Shows the diff before you allow an edit, and answers agent questions in the notch.
  • An on-device voice assistant that understands English and Tamil, and can only deny, never approve.
  • Looks after your day: WhatsApp greetings and replies, a morning brief, service status.

Where Nova is catching up

  • Usage and plan limits: coming in v0.8.
  • A desktop companion mode and accessories (like headphones while music plays): coming in v0.9.
  • Fewer agents than the widest apps (6 versus about 30), though any tool can report to Nova.

Already using a notch app?

NotchNook, Alcove and The Boring Notch are great for music, files and widgets. Nova focuses on your agents, your Mac's safety and your day, and you can run it alongside them.
