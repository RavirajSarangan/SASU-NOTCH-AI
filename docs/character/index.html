Nova 2 character plan

Classic Nova is ten painted pictures with live eyes on top. Nova 2 keeps the same face: white fur, cat ears, the black visor with glowing eyes and the triangle chest light. It is drawn from shapes in code, with joints and springs, so every part moves on its own.

Nova 2 breathes, fidgets, looks at your cursor, purrs when you pet it, knocks when an agent waits for you, and dances on the beat of your music.

What makes it feel alive

Motion is built in layers on top of each other. Every layer is small; together they stop Nova from ever looking like a still picture.

  1. Mood poseEach of the 18 moods sets targets for every joint.
  2. SpringsEach joint has its own spring. Eyes move first, then the head and body; ears and tail settle last.
  3. Idle lifeBreathing, blinks and double blinks, eye darts, and a weight shift.
  4. FidgetsAn ear flick, tail curl, look around, paw groom or visor hum every 6 to 14 seconds, never the same twice in a row.
  5. ReactionsHop, "aha!", startle, flinch, cheer, nod, knock. Each has wind-up, action, follow-through and settle.
  6. BridgesGestures between moods: waking up startles, thinking to success says "aha!", and an error makes Nova flinch.
  7. Look and touchNova watches your cursor, purrs when petted, and dangles when dragged.
  8. ScenesAn animated background per mood, crossfading as the mood changes.

Dances to your music

When Spotify or Music plays, Nova puts on headphones and dances on the real beat: head bobs, sways, ear bops and paw pumps, with a spin on the drop. It stops and turns to you if an agent needs you.

Reacts to agent work

Paws type as fast as the agent works. When tests fail, a paw goes over the visor; a commit stamps the chest light. If an approval waits, Nova looks at you, then waves, then knocks on the notch.

Session sparks

Every live agent session is a spark in its own colour, orbiting Nova. When one needs you, it pulses and Nova points at it. Clicking it jumps to that terminal.

Petting and play

Rub Nova's head and it purrs and leans in, with hearts. A click is a boop. Three clicks make it dizzy. In desktop mode, drag it and it dangles; shake it and it gets dizzy.

Guardian and daily rhythm

When Nova Guard finds a threat, Nova's ears go back and a shield sweeps across its visor; on "all clear" it shakes it off. It stretches in the morning, suggests a break late at night, and cheers your streaks.

Design rules

  • One silhouetteTwo ears and a round head must read as Nova even as a black shape at 24 pt.
  • The face does the talkingEyes carry the mood first, ears second, paws third. A mood must still read in the notch, where only the head shows.
  • Follow-through, not bounceEars and tail lag behind on soft springs. While you work, nothing loops faster than once a second.
  • Quiet by defaultBig motion only for events: a task done, an approval, an error, a drop in your song. Reduce Motion keeps only the mood and the beat bob of the ears.
  • You come firstAn approval, a Guard alert or an error always interrupts the dance or a fidget.
  • Private by designBeat detection runs on the Mac. The audio is analysed in memory and never stored or sent.
  • Cheap to drawOne Canvas draw per frame, 30 fps in the notch, paused when hidden. Idle CPU stays under 5%, dancing under 8%.
  • Our own designNo shapes, names or sounds from other apps. Classic stays available in Settings.

How we build it

Each step ends with something you can look at. Nothing becomes the default until you approve it.

  1. This prototypeThe motion, ideas, dance and backgrounds, tuned here first because it is fast to change.

  2. Rig maths in SwiftNovaKit/Character: springs, mood targets, reactions, fidgets and bridges, with this page's numbers, plus BeatTracker and Choreographer, tested on click tracks at 90, 120 and 140 BPM.
    Tests
  3. Renderer and backgroundsNovaRigRenderer and SceneBackgroundView in SwiftUI Canvas. Turnaround and motion sheets rendered by the real app.

  4. Real events and musicAgents, approvals, sessions, Guard and clicks drive the reactions. A Core Audio tap on Spotify or Music provides the beat, after one permission prompt.
